The night-sky landscape and Moon photograph were supplied by the portfolio owner.
`night-sky.webp` is the complete source photograph. `rocks_silhouette.png` is its
transparent foreground, and `sky-plate.webp` extends the sky behind the removed
ridge so parallax never reveals a duplicate mountain. Regenerate both derived
assets with `python scripts/make-rocks-cutout.py <source-image> public/hero/rocks_silhouette.png public/hero/sky-plate.webp`.
The Moon's black background was made transparent.
